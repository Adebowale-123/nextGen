Provably fair draws

You don't have to take our word that a draw was random. Every draw can be independently checked.

  1. Commit. When a draw opens we generate a secret 256-bit server seed and publish its SHA-256 hash. Once published, we can't change the seed without the hash changing.
  2. Player entropy. When sales close, a client seed is computed from the signatures of every ticket sold. It didn't exist when we committed, so we couldn't have picked a seed to suit it.
  3. Draw. Winning numbers come from HMAC-SHA256(server_seed, "client_seed:nonce:round"), read 4 bytes at a time with rejection sampling (no modulo bias), skipping repeats.
  4. Reveal. After the draw we publish the server seed. Anyone can hash it, compare it with the commitment, and recompute the numbers. Every results page has a one-click verifier that runs in your browser.

Your tickets are signed too

Each ticket gets a unique serial and a cryptographic signature over your account, the draw, the purchase time and your numbers. If anything about a ticket were changed after purchase, the signature would no longer match — your ticket page shows the check.

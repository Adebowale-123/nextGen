How it works

  1. Press Play Game. It costs {{ game.ticket_price|coins }} and you instantly get {{ game.pick_count }} numbers from 1–{{ game.number_max }}.
  2. When the game closes, we spin {{ game.pick_count }} winning numbers.
  3. The tickets closest to the winning numbers win: the top ticket wins the grand prize of {{ game.grand_prize|money }} and the next closest win {{ game.consolation_prize|money }} each.
  4. Prizes are paid in naira straight into your winnings, ready to withdraw.

The number of prizes in each game depends on how many people play. Tickets with more matching numbers rank higher; ties are settled by a fair random draw you can verify on the results page.

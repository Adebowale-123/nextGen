How it works

  1. Press Play Game. It costs {{ game.ticket_price|coins }} and you instantly get {{ game.pick_count }} numbers from 1–{{ game.number_max }}.
  2. When the game closes, we spin {{ game.pick_count }} winning numbers.
  3. Match all {{ game.pick_count }} to win the grand prize of {{ game.grand_prize|coins }}.
  4. Players who match numbers win {{ game.consolation_prize|coins }} consolation prizes, best matches first, until the prize pool is used up.

Prize rules in full
  • {{ game.prize_pool_percent }}% of each game's sales goes into the prize pool.
  • The grand prize of {{ game.grand_prize|coins }} is guaranteed for anyone matching all {{ game.pick_count }} numbers.
  • If nobody wins the grand prize, the whole pool goes to consolation winners.
  • When more players qualify than the pool can pay, those with more matches win first. Ties are decided by a random order fixed by the spin, which anyone can verify.
  • Prize money too small for another consolation prize is added to the next game's pool.
  • Winnings are paid straight into your wallet when the spin is done.
